How the time works

Counting continues while the dashboard is closed. Videos and games count while the session is active and unlocked.

A locked screen, sleep and time on other accounts do not use this allowance. The limit renews at midnight.

When time runs out, this session is signed out and its programs are closed. Save your work before the final warning.

A new sign-in is signed out again while the allowance is used up. Accounts without a rule, and administrators, are not affected.
